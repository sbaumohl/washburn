About

Washburn is an automated research project. It crawls publicly reachable web pages on a set of operator-chosen sites, screens the text of each page with a classifier, and sends passages that score as potentially serious to a second, stronger model for review.

Automated output is not a conclusion. Every report on this site has been read and approved by a person before publication. Reports describe what a page says and what the reviewers could and could not establish; they are not legal findings, and a page's operator may have context that is not visible from the page itself.

If you believe a report is wrong or about a site you run, contact the operator at the address listed on the domain.

This site shows only published reports and aggregate counts. Unreviewed material is never shown here.
